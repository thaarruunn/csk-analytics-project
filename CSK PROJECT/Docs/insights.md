INSIGHT NO. 10

MODERN IPL SCORING EXPECTATIONS MAY HAVE CHANGED FASTER THAN CSK'S BATTING APPROACH.

proof(s) :

several recent IPL seasons have seen teams regularly score 220-250 runs.

however CSK's batting outputs often remained closer to traditional IPL scoring levels (180 ish).

observation :

the batting KPIs analysed in this project did not show a strong relationship with season success.

however these metrics do not directly measure batting intent, strike rate acceleration or adaptation to changing league scoring trends.

conclusion :

while batting alone does not appear to explain CSK's decline, further analysis may be required to determine whether CSK adapted effectively to the increasingly aggressive batting environment of modern IPL cricket.
