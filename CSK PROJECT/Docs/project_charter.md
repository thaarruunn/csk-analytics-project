The Evolution of CSK's Winning Identity:
A Data-Driven Analysis of Success, Decline, and Rebuild (2018–2026)

--Background about CSK 

Chennai Super Kings have historically been one of the most successful
franchises in IPL history.

However, recent seasons have raised questions about whether the team has lost the characteristics that contributed to earlier success.

This project investigates how CSK's performance evolved between 2018 and 2026 using ball-by-ball IPL data.

-- Business Question

CSK experienced championship success in 2018, 2021 and 2023.

Recent seasons have shown signs of decline.

The causes of this decline are often discussed qualitatively by fans and
analysts.

This project seeks to evaluate these claims using data and identify evidence based explanations.

--Project Objective 

Use historical IPL ball-by-ball data to identify:

- Drivers of CSK success
- Drivers of CSK decline
- Changes in Chepauk conditions
- Changes in tactical identity
- Future rebuild priorities

--Key Research Questions 

1. Why did CSK succeed in title-winning seasons?

2. Why did CSK decline after 2023?

3. Has Chepauk changed as a venue?

4. Has CSK lost home advantage?

5. What characteristics should future CSK squads prioritize?
