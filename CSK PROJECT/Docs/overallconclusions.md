WHAT DID WE LEARN?

by performing data analysis across multiple IPL seasons, we learnt that successful CSK teams were not built around a single metric.

instead, success appears to be driven by a combination of factors such as:

* taking wickets consistently
* taking wickets in the powerplay
* maintaining economical bowling attacks
* converting defending opportunities into victories
* utilizing home advantage effectively
* receiving contributions from multiple players rather than relying on a single star performer

the analysis suggests that championship seasons were generally characterised by strong overall team performance rather than excellence in one area alone.

WHAT EXPLAINS SUCCESS?

the following factors showed the strongest relationship with successful CSK seasons:

* higher total wickets
* stronger powerplay wicket-taking ability
* lower spin economy rates
* lower death-over economy rates
* stronger defending win percentages
* stronger home performances

successful CSK teams consistently controlled matches through bowling pressure and early breakthroughs while also converting key moments into victories.

WHAT EXPLAINS DECLINE?

the decline appears to be driven by a combination of factors rather than one single weakness.

key indicators include:

* fewer wickets taken overall
* reduced powerplay wicket-taking ability
* worsening spin economy rates
* worsening death-over economy rates
* weaker defending records
* collapse of home advantage in certain seasons

the data suggests that recent teams have struggled to replicate the bowling effectiveness and match control displayed by championship-winning squads.

while batting metrics did not show strong relationships with overall success, changing IPL scoring trends and batting intent may require further investigation.

WHAT CANNOT BE EXPLAINED BY THE DATA?

the dataset does not capture several important cricketing factors.

these include:

* injuries and player availability
* leadership and captaincy decisions
* auction and squad-building strategy
* player form and confidence
* dressing-room environment
* team morale
* off-field factors

for example, injuries played a significant role in recent seasons but could not be directly measured within the dataset.

therefore, the findings of this project should be interpreted alongside cricketing context rather than viewed as the sole explanation for team performance.
